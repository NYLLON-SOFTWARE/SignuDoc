# Risk assessment

A starting risk assessment for SignuDoc in Part 11 mode. Score each risk for your own records and processes. The scores below assume an ordinary GxP use, such as approving SOPs or batch records, and should be changed if yours is different.

**Severity** (S): 1 = no effect on product quality or patient safety, 3 = could affect a record's integrity, 5 = could hide a quality problem or let an unauthorized signature stand.
**Likelihood** (L) after the controls: 1 = remote, 3 = possible, 5 = expected.
**Detectability** (D): 1 = would certainly be found, 5 = would probably not be found.
A risk priority (S × L × D) of 27 or more needs a further control or a documented acceptance.
